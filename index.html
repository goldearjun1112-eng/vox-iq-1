On-device AI model

Notes are analyzed by a small language model (Qwen2.5-0.5B) that runs entirely on this device — nothing is sent to a server, no API key needed. It downloads once (~300MB) and is cached by the browser after that. Quality is noticeably rougher than a large cloud model, especially on longer or heavily Hindi/Marathi-mixed transcripts.
